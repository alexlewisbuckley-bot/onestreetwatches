What we can find

Current-production references with a waiting list. Discontinued pieces. A specific dial, a specific year, a full set when you have been offered watch-only for months. If it trades, we can usually reach it.

01

Tell us the reference

The more specific the better — dial colour, bracelet, year, whether papers matter to you, and the ceiling you will not go past.

02

We go looking

Our own network first, then trusted dealers in Dubai, London, Geneva and Hong Kong. You are not paying us to browse public listings.

03

Real options, real prices

We come back with what actually exists, with photographs and condition notes, and what each one will cost you delivered.

04

Authenticated before it reaches you

Anything we source goes across our own bench first. If it fails, you never see an invoice.

What it costs

Nothing to start, and nothing if we come up empty. When we do find it you pay the agreed price for the watch — our margin is inside that number and we tell you what it is before you commit.

Why this works

Most of what trades at this level never appears on a public listing. It moves between dealers, collectors and a handful of people who know who owns what.

Four years of buying and selling in Dubai and the UK buys you access to that. We are not searching the same websites you already have open — we are asking people directly, and they answer because we buy from them regularly and pay on time.

What we will not do

We will not take a deposit and go quiet. We will not present a watch we have not seen photographs of. And we will not tell you something is rare when it is sitting in three windows on the same street.
