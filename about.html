Why we started

Because buying a serious watch had become an exercise in being managed. Waiting lists you could not see the end of, prices that moved depending on who was asking, and a lot of people selling watches they had never physically held.

We wanted the opposite of that. Own the stock. Open it up. Say what it costs and why. If a watch has been polished, say so. If the papers are gone, say so. If a client would be better off buying elsewhere, say that too.

What that looks like in practice

Every watch on this site is physically in our possession, in Dubai or the UK. Every one has passed 41 checks at our own bench, and the sheet is filed against its serial. Every price is set against live transaction data, and we will show you the comparables if you ask.

It is not a complicated idea. It is just unusual enough in this trade to be worth building a business on.

The boutique

Gold & Diamond Park, Building 5 — travertine, oak, and a case you are welcome to sit in front of for as long as you like.

We built a room rather than a shop. There is coffee, there are chairs, and there is no expectation that you buy anything. Plenty of our best clients came in for a look and bought something eighteen months later.

Two countries

We hold stock in both the UAE and the UK, which means most clients can have a watch in their hands the next day without customs, duty or a three-week wait. Part-exchange works across both, in either direction.

The bench

Two Swiss-trained watchmakers, in-house. Nothing is sent away without telling you, and nothing is listed before it has been opened, timed and pressure-tested.
