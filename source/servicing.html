How a service runs

01

Drop off or free collection

Bring it to Dubai, or we arrange insured collection anywhere in the UAE or the UK at our cost.

02

Assessment and a fixed quote

We open it, tell you what it needs and what it does not, and quote a firm number. Nothing proceeds until you agree.

03

On the bench

Strip-down, clean, new gaskets, lubrication, reassembly and regulation across six positions.

04

Timing and pressure test

Rate, amplitude and beat error recorded before and after. Dry pressure test to the stated depth.

05

Back to you, warranted

Returned insured with the timing sheet and a 24-month warranty on the work.
