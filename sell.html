What lifts an offer

Box and papers together are usually worth 8–12% over watch-only on a modern reference. An unpolished case, original bracelet links, and a service history all move the number up. We will tell you honestly if a service before sale would earn more than it costs.

Why sell to a dealer

Selling privately can net a little more on paper. It also means meeting strangers with a six-figure object, handling payment fraud risk, and waiting — often months.

We take the risk instead. You get a firm number, insured collection, and cleared funds the same day. For most people the difference between the two routes is smaller than the difference in sleep.

What we do with it

Every watch we buy goes through the same 41-point bench process as anything else we sell, and is photographed and catalogued before it goes back into the case. Nothing is flipped unseen.
